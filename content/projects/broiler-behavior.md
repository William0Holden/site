- Built a spatiotemporal video encoder with metadata fusion (X3D 3D-CNN + YOLO tracking) for
  automated broiler-chicken behavior recognition, trained on 200+ hours of smartphone farm footage.
- Developed motion-correction algorithms to normalize avian kinematics and remove human-handling
  bias, enabling reliable velocity and acceleration metrics from handheld video.
- **1st place**, UGA Artificial Intelligence in Livestock and Poultry Farming Conference (poster).

**Publication:** Holden, W.O., Li, G., Agyemang-Duah, E., Poku, Adenyo, Osei-Amponsah, Aggrey.
"Spatiotemporal video encoder integrated with metadata fusion for action recognition and behavior
analysis." *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture* 253 (2026), 112159 (IF 10.3).
