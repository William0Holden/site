Class projects, not production systems. The larger, more original ones have their own pages: the cinema booking system
came from software engineering (CSCI 4050), BiteButler from UGA Hacks 8, and the translation benchmark from PHIL 8400.

- **Color Game (CSCI 4300, web programming, spring 2024):** a team of four built a full-stack game where players log
  in, pick a team, and turn a button their team's color, with a stats page. Next/React, Express, Mongoose, and JWT.
- **Data science (CSCI 6380):** term-project work on retail sales regression, forest fire prediction, and inflation
  forecasting with scikit-learn and Keras, plus PCA, k-nearest neighbors, and ROC analysis.
- **Computer vision (CSCI 8820):** connected components, peakiness thresholding, multi-scale Laplacian-of-Gaussian edge
  detection in NumPy, and a fingerprint-enhancement experiment with CLAHE and Gabor filters.
- **Artificial intelligence (CSCI 6550, fall 2022):** minimax with alpha-beta pruning for the BLOBS board game, and
  search solvers including A* and heuristics for the eight puzzle and water jugs, built on aima-python.
- **Systems and networks:** a socket web server and tcpdump and traceroute parsing (CSCI 4760), a small Unix shell and
  head, tail, and wc in C (CSCI 1730), and MIPS assembly (CSCI 4720).
- **Software development (CSCI 1302, 2720):** a JavaFX image gallery on the iTunes API, minesweeper, and data structures
  with sorting experiments in Java.
