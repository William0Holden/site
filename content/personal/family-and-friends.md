He grew up in Columbus, Georgia, and has a twin sister. The family is close: he talks to his mom and his twin nearly every day. The family dog is Daisy, a black dog.

His closest friends are from college: a gaming crew, and the people he travels with. When they
turned up to cheer for his thesis defense, his first words afterward were "so, you guys wanna play video games?"
He also runs a media server that about eight friends and family share.

He keeps the people in his life off the internet, so no names here.
